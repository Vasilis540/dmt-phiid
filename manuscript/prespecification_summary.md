## Changes made after specification and before the result they govern

- **Tier-2 threshold moved from the per-subject mean ρ to the group-mean-series ρ** (e46df8a, 13 Sep 12:29). Reason: the simulation had validated R on pooled window means, which is the group-mean-series version; the per-subject ρ over 9 windows was never simulated, and averaging noisy per-subject correlations attenuates (the LZ check had shown −0.24 per-subject against −0.83 group-mean series on the same data). The per-subject mean was kept as an existence test, not thresholded. The Primary B ts_gsr W = 60 run was in progress and unread when this was committed; no real windowed result existed.
- **Effective sample size moved from ts_demean to ts_gsr as the deciding value** (both the first draft and the revision are within 18ad8b4, 12 Sep 21:43; the record states the first draft named ts_demean). Reason: ts_gsr is the analysis stream for every other result, so the deciding precision figure should come from the same stream; the smaller ts_demean value was kept as a required sensitivity. Before the non-stationary run; no real windowed result existed.
- **Real-data primary decay windows moved from 5–14 to 6–14** (febf599, 13 Sep 11:32). Reason: the global fit showed an injection response at bins 8–10 in placebo (rtr bump at bins 8–9 on ts_demean, sts dip at bin 10 on ts_gsr), so window 5 (bins 9–10) is onset-contaminated; 5–14 kept as sensitivity and as the window set on which the simulation tier is assigned. Before any real windowed run and before the 20,000-run verdict.
- **W = 60 branch taken after the log-det correction check** (constant set at 8554b3d on the prediction; step 2 executed at febf599, 13 Sep 11:32). Reason: the correction shifted every condition by the same constant and changed the differential bias by exactly zero, as predicted, so the W = 30 criterion failed on its second clause and the pre-specified fallback applied. No real windowed result existed (first at e46df8a).

## Predictions that failed

- **Redundancy prediction** (33f0b33). Predicted: rtr rises under DMT with a mirrored time course. Happened: on ts_gsr rtr fell with sts (DiD −0.0091, positive in 4 of 14, r = 0.66 between the two time courses); on ts_demean the group-mean rtr DiD was +0.0137 but positive in 7 of 14, peaked at the injection bin, and coincided with a placebo bump. Redundancy-dominance not supported on either variant.
- **W = 30 shrinkage ratio** (febf599). Predicted: W = 30 DiD at one-third to two-thirds of W = 60. Happened: 0.85 (−0.0686 vs −0.0809), outside the range; same sign, so the inconsistency criterion was not met. The follow-up AR-shift condition (f3b435d) predicted a ratio near 0.85 if shift type was the cause; it gave 0.60, so that explanation failed too, and the diagnostic showed no simulated family matches the real dependence structure.
- **Noise-floor prediction** (18ad8b4). Predicted: the adjacent-pair criterion marginal or failing, late-decay pairs near chance, early pairs correctly signed. Happened: early pairs correctly signed as predicted; at 2,000 runs two pairs per condition appeared wrong in expectation, which the 20,000-run repeat showed to be sampling error; every decay pair was correctly signed in expectation at 20,000 runs.
- **Tier-1 marginality prediction for the repeat** (18ad8b4). Predicted: coupling S ≤ 0.89 and plausibly < 0.80, tier 1 fail, tier 2 pass. Happened (dbeca36): all four deciding point estimates passed (coupling 0.923 and 0.887), but both noise-correlation intervals straddled 0.80, so tier 2 was assigned by the escalation rule; tier 1 reported as marginal.

## What this record is and is not

The record is a git-tracked local file, `manuscript/analysis_record.md` (named `CLAUDE.md` until 14 Sep 2026, when it was moved with `git mv` so that `git log --follow` carries its full history; the content was not changed), in the analysis repository; it is not an external pre-registration and was not deposited with any registry. What it establishes is ordering: each decision, prediction and rule is in a commit whose sequence in the history precedes the commit carrying the result it governs, and each results table carries the SHA of the code that produced it. It does not establish that the record could not have been rewritten before the repository was made public; the repository has been public since 13 Sep 2026, and commits before that date are attested only by their sequence and content. The record was written over two days.
