## Figure 2

Per-subject difference-in-differences (DiD) in whole-brain mean sts: (post − pre) on the DMT run minus (post − pre) on the placebo run, pre = windows 1–4 (bins 1–8), post = primary windows 6–14 (bins 11–28), W = 60, N = 14. Left: ts_gsr; right: ts_demean. One dot per subject (subject index labelled), ordered by value; the horizontal line is the group mean and the band its subject-bootstrap 95 % CI (10,000 draws, seed 20261120); zero is marked. The p value in each title is from the exact sign-flip permutation test across the 14 subjects (all 2^14 = 16,384 assignments, two-sided). The pre-specified direction was positive. Group mean, CI and p are read from `results/primary_b_<variant>_win60.csv`; the per-subject values are derived from `results/atoms_win60_115regions-all_<variant>_window.npy` with the same window sets, and their mean is asserted equal to the table value before drawing.

Exploratory regional map of the DMT synergy change, ts_gsr, Robustness A global fit, in two panels sharing one x axis (left: visual, somatomotor, dorsal-attention and salience/ventral-attention networks; right: limbic, control, default and subcortex). This analysis was specified after the primary result and is exploratory; no motion handling and no temporal null are applied at the regional level.
