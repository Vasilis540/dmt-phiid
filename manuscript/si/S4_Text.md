# S4 Text. The COBIDAS reporting checklist for MRI, filled for this secondary analysis

Supporting information for the paper "The Gaussian-MMI synergy atom of integrated information decomposition is mostly self-prediction". The items follow the OHBM Committee on Best Practices in Data Analysis and Sharing report for MRI (COBIDAS, released in 2016; its scope, seven domains and checklists are described in the commentary of Nichols et al., 2017, *Nature Neuroscience* 20, 299–303), sections D (experimental design), A (acquisition), P (preprocessing), S (statistical modelling and inference), R (results) and Sh (data sharing); the item wording is paraphrased from the report's checklist, and the numbering is the report's section order, not its exact item numbers. This paper is a secondary analysis of parcellated, preprocessed regional time series released by the data authors (Singleton et al., 2025; acquired by Timmermann et al., 2023), pseudonymised (S1 Text): no data were collected and no image was processed here, so every acquisition and image-preprocessing item is answered by pointing to the data authors' reports, and "not applicable" below always means "not done in this study; reported by the data authors where the item applies to their acquisition". What the data authors report was checked, on 1 October 2026, against the main text and SI Appendix of Timmermann et al. (2023) and the main text, Supplementary Information and Reporting Summary of Singleton et al. (2025) (record, "The cold reads of 1 October 2026"; the claim record of the revision that followed); "not stated in the sources read" means stated in none of those five documents, which is a statement about what this analysis could cite, not a claim about those papers. Status codes: **reported** (with the location in the main text or the supporting information), **not applicable** (with the reason), **not reported** (with what is missing). Locations name the sections of the main text (Results 1–7 and the subsections of Materials and methods) and the supporting files as they stand in this version.

S1 Text: two testing days two weeks apart, a single-blind design with the order counterbalanced, half the participants receiving placebo first (Timmermann et al., 2023; Singleton et al., 2025); participants were blind to the order and the study personnel were not (Singleton et al., 2025, Methods and Reporting Summary). Each subject's own order is not among the released files, so no result is split by order (Limitations). |
| D9 Behavioural performance | not applicable | No behavioural task; the subjective intensity ratings are described (S1 Text; S2 Table), and the main text states the outcome of the criterion that uses them (Results 2). |

| P2 Distortion, slice-timing and motion correction | not applicable | Despiking, slice-timing correction and motion correction (AFNI's 3dDespike, 3dTshift and 3dvolreg, each volume registered to the volume most similar to all others): Timmermann et al. (2023); Singleton et al. (2025, Methods and Reporting Summary). Distortion correction is not stated in the sources read. |
| P3 Intra- and inter-subject registration, template | not applicable | Brain extraction (BET), rigid-body registration to the subject's anatomical scan and nonlinear registration to the 2 mm MNI template (SyN, ANTS): Timmermann et al. (2023); Singleton et al. (2025, Methods and Reporting Summary). The parcellation into 100 cortical and 16 subcortical regions is the data authors' (Singleton et al., 2025); the sources read do not state the space in which it was applied. |
| P4 Spatial smoothing | not applicable | 6 mm FWHM (3dBlurInMask), before the band-pass: Timmermann et al. (2023); Singleton et al. (2025, Methods and Reporting Summary). |
| P5 Temporal filtering | reported | Methods, Dataset: the released series are band-limited to 0.01–0.08 Hz; the consequence for lag-1 autocorrelation is stated in Results 7 (r₁ ≈ 0.82 for white noise at TR = 2 s, and r₁ ≥ 0.54 for any series confined to the band) and, for the first of the two values, in S1 Text. |
| P6 Nuisance regression and denoising (motion parameters, CompCor, scrubbing, global signal) | reported for what varies here; otherwise not applicable | Two released variants are analysed and named at every result: with global signal regression (`ts_gsr`, primary) and demeaned without it (`ts_demean`, sensitivity) — Methods, Dataset. The remaining denoising steps are the data authors' (Timmermann et al., 2023; Singleton et al., 2025, Methods and Reporting Summary): volumes with framewise displacement above 0.4 replaced by the mean of the surrounding volumes (volume censoring), linear and quadratic detrending, nine nuisance regressors (six motion parameters; the ventricles, the draining veins and the local white matter), each band-pass filtered like the data, and, in Singleton et al. (2025) alone, global signal regression last (the `ts_gsr` variant); what the replacement does to r₁ is examined in S3 Text §5 and stated in Limitations. HRF deconvolution (rsHRF 1.7.0) was applied here only as a post hoc exploration (Methods, Remedies; S2 Text, which gives its parameters). |
| P7 Parcellation / atlas | reported | Methods, Dataset: Schaefer-100 parcels plus 16 subcortical ones; one region, mean-filled for one subject and confirmed as such by the data authors, dropped for all subjects and both conditions (S1 Text names it), leaving 115 regions and 6,555 pairs. |
| P8 Data quality checks on the derivatives | reported | S1 Text: one non-finite TR, the last of subject 3's placebo run, at the end of window 14, dropped (S3 Text §5); subject alignment across files checked (record, "Subject alignment across files"); `scripts/00_verify.py` checks shapes and finiteness before every run. |
| P9 Motion quantification and handling | reported | Methods, Inference: window-mean framewise displacement regressed out within each subject and condition and the DiD recomputed on the residuals (S1 Text); the FD DiD itself and the FD-residualised DiD are reported (Table 2), with what the residualisation removes besides motion (Results 2); the release's replaced volumes per window and their relation to r₁: S3 Text §5; Limitations. |
