# S1 Text. The original pre-specified analysis and its results

Supporting information for "The Gaussian-MMI synergy atom of integrated information decomposition is mostly self-prediction". The original analysis is the one the study set out to run; its full write-up is kept as a record in `manuscript/draft.md` (superseded, not cited), its supplementary tables are S1–S8 Tables of `manuscript/supplementary.md`, and its decisions are listed commit by commit in `manuscript/prespecification_summary.md`, which covers that analysis only. Every number below is quoted from those files and from `results/`.

**The hypothesis and the rule.** The study was pre-specified in a git-tracked record (`manuscript/analysis_record.md`) as a confirmatory test of whether whole-brain synergy (the ΦID atom sts under the Gaussian MMI estimator at lag one TR) is up-regulated under N,N-dimethyltryptamine, against the workspace account's prediction that synergy indexes conscious level (Luppi et al., 2024); the predicted direction was an increase. The hypothesis is the record's opening section, committed with the first scripts (44cec4f, 12 September 2026). A directional-failure rule — a significant decrease is reported as a refutation and not reframed — was written after a 115-region global fit had shown a decrease and entered the repository in the same commit as that result (33f0b33, 13 September 2026); the windowed estimator, the window sets, the nulls and the motion rule were fixed before the windowed result existed (e16ebba to e46df8a). The git record establishes ordering, not blindness; the full history is S5 Text.

**The windowed test.** Primary B: whole-brain mean MMI-sts in 14 non-overlapping 60-TR windows, model refit per window, the pre/post difference-in-differences (DiD) of windows 6–14 against 1–4, DMT minus placebo, per subject; exact sign-flip permutation over the 14 subjects, subject-bootstrap 95 % CI, a phase-randomised temporal null, framewise-displacement (FD) reporting and an FD-residualised recomputation. Result (`results/primary_b_ts_gsr_win60.csv`): DiD −0.0809 nats [−0.1261, −0.0377], sign-flip p = 0.0038, phase-randomised p = 0.0020, negative in 13 of 14 subjects, −7.0 % of the pre-injection DMT mean; FD-residualised −0.0649 [−0.0966, −0.0289], p = 0.0048; without global signal regression −0.1031 [−0.1558, −0.0522], p = 0.0026. Under the directional-failure rule this refuted the up-regulation hypothesis. Total time-delayed mutual information fell with sts (TDMI DiD −0.1037 on ts_gsr).

**The intensity-tracking criterion and its controls.** A tier-2 criterion (the group-mean sts series against the intensity ratings over the decay windows, |ρ| ≥ 0.80, with a time-in-scanner control on the placebo run and an FD-residualised recomputation that had to pass that control in full) was pre-specified with two void rules. The tracking was significant and negative, and it was void under its controls (S2 Table); the claimed tier is 3 (step contrast plus methods contribution). The ratings were collected in a later session of the same day than the analysed runs (Timmermann et al., 2023; Singleton et al., 2025), which is why they enter no result of the main text.

**The redundancy prediction.** Recorded before rtr was inspected: under a redundancy-dominance reading of an sts decrease, rtr should rise under DMT with a time course mirroring the sts decrease. It did not — on ts_gsr rtr fell with sts (DiD −0.0078, positive in 4 of 14 subjects), and the reading is unsupported on either variant (record, 84ea657).

**Exploratory regional analysis.** The regional DiD map (the mean over a region's pairs of the global-fit local sts) was negative in 114 of 115 regions on both variants; seven regions survived FDR on ts_gsr and 19 on ts_demean, all negative and all cortical, four common to both (S3 Table). The workspace comparison found no concentration of the decrease in the network proxy of the synergistic workspace: workspace minus non-workspace mean regional DiD −0.0095 [−0.0230, +0.0050], p = 0.216, on ts_gsr and −0.0050 [−0.0225, +0.0120], p = 0.590, on ts_demean, with the same picture under the named-subregion, subcortex-inclusive, gateway and broadcaster variants (S4 Table). The receptor-map correlations (five serotonin maps, spin test over 10,000 parcel rotations) survived no correction on either variant (S5 Table). All exploratory.

**EEG Lempel-Ziv check.** The prediction recorded before computation, that TDMI anti-correlates with EEG Lempel-Ziv complexity within the DMT run, held on both variants: per-subject Spearman ρ across 28 bins −0.2416 [−0.4011, −0.0765], one-sided p = 0.0020 against the phase-randomised LZ null on ts_gsr, nothing on the placebo run (S6 Table; global functional connectivity per bin set in S7 Table). Both series are drug-locked, so the check establishes coherence across modalities, not tracking beyond the drug.

**Robustness across estimators.** The W = 30 positive control gave the same sign at 0.85 of the W = 60 magnitude (−0.0686 [−0.1084, −0.0293], p = 0.0042), and the pre-specified inconsistency criterion (opposite sign or larger magnitude) was not met. The global fit (Robustness A) gave −0.0801 [−0.1303, −0.0310], p = 0.0071, on ts_gsr and −0.1035 [−0.1678, −0.0357], p = 0.0132, on ts_demean. A placebo-fitted model applied out of sample to the DMT run (Robustness C) gave DMT − PCB −0.1110 [−0.1803, −0.0593], p = 0.0005, over bins 15–28, and a within-DMT step of −0.0508 [−0.1047, +0.0137], p = 0.1338. The sensitivity window set (5–14) is S1 Table; the post-hoc proportionality check is S8 Table.

**What happened next.** An adversarial review on 14 September 2026, with its own computations, established that the decrease was carried by the self-prediction atoms and reproduced by the lag-1 autocorrelation contrast; the study was reframed as an account of the estimator (main text). Nothing above was relabelled by that reframing.
