# S1 Text. The original pre-specified analysis and its results

Supporting information for "The Gaussian-MMI synergy atom of integrated information decomposition is mostly self-prediction". The original analysis is the one the study set out to run; its full write-up is kept as a record in `manuscript/draft.md` (superseded, not cited), its supplementary tables are S1–S8 Tables of `manuscript/supplementary.md`, and its decisions are listed commit by commit in `manuscript/prespecification_summary.md`, which covers that analysis only. Every number below is quoted from those files and from `results/`, except that the intervals of means over subjects are the inverted sign-flip intervals that replaced the pre-specified subject-bootstrap percentile intervals on 23 September 2026 (`notes/review_results/partB/inference_revision.csv`; S17 Table gives both); the intervals marked "percentile" are those with no saved per-subject vector.

**Ethics, data and estimator (moved from the main text's Methods on 23 September 2026).** The original study was approved by the National Research Ethics Committee London – Brent and the Health Research Authority and conducted under the revised Declaration of Helsinki (2000), the ICH Good Clinical Practice guidelines and the NHS Research Governance Framework, sponsored by Imperial College London under a Home Office licence for research with Schedule 1 drugs, with written informed consent from all participants. This wording follows the approval statement of Timmermann et al. (2023), which names the committee but gives no reference number. The present work is a secondary analysis of pseudonymised, parcellated regional time series and per-TR framewise displacement released by the data authors (Singleton et al., 2025); no new data were collected and no participant was contacted. The quantities used carry no demographic, image or identifying field. The released ratings table (`intensity_ratings.mat` in the source release) carries subject codes — an S, a two-digit subject number and two letters — which this analysis does not use and which the repository's tracked files do not contain (they were removed from them on 15 September 2026); the codes remain in the repository's git history, which carries nothing the public source release does not (S5 Text). Whether the two letters identify anyone is a question for the data authors [TK: to be confirmed with C. Timmermann and S. P. Singleton; if they do, the history is rewritten before the preprint]. DMT (20 mg DMT fumarate) was injected intravenously over 30 s at TR 240, on a 3 T scanner. Drug order was counterbalanced across the two testing days, two weeks apart, half the participants receiving placebo first (Timmermann et al., 2023); the analysed runs are the first, task-free session of each day. The intensity ratings supplied with the data enter no result of the main text; their 28 bins of 30 TRs are the global fit's averaging unit, and framewise displacement (FD) per TR is supplied. Both preprocessing variants are band-limited to 0.01–0.08 Hz, which alone imposes r₁ ≈ 0.82 on white noise at TR 2 s. Region 20 (0-based; LH_DorsAttn_Post_6), constant across the DMT run of subject 8 and confirmed by the data authors as a mean-filled replacement, was dropped for all subjects and both conditions before any result was computed, leaving 115 regions and 6,555 pairs; one non-finite TR was dropped. ΦID atoms were computed with phyid (Imperial-MIND-lab, 2026, at the pinned commit), calc_PhiID(kind='gaussian', tau=1) with redundancy 'MMI' or 'CCS': a Gaussian model of (x_t, y_t, x_{t+1}, y_{t+1}), sixteen local atoms per time point, in nats; whole-brain values are means over the 6,555 pairs. Windowed estimator, W = 60: 14 non-overlapping 60-TR windows per run, each an independent fit on its own samples; W = 30 (28 windows) as a second. Global fit: one Gaussian fitted to the full run, local atoms averaged into the 28 bins. The windowed estimator's bias was characterised before any windowed result on simulated VAR(1) processes with analytic ground truth (2,000 replicate windows per setting; S3 Text, section 6). Window 5 was excluded before any windowed result because both arms of the global fit showed an injection response at bins 8–10; the sensitivity set (windows 5–14) was fixed with the primary set on 13 September 2026, before any windowed result (record, "Pre-registered analysis choices", the decay-windows item); the early and late sets (6–9, 10–14) and two trend corrections were added after the primary result. A phase-randomised surrogate of the TR-resolution whole-brain local series per subject and condition (1,000 surrogates; Theiler et al., 1992), re-averaged into the same windows, is reported in the tables as a stationarity check of the derived series and carries no inference; motion control regressed window-mean values on window-mean FD within each subject and condition and recomputed the DiD on the residuals. The lag variants use the lag-τ four-vector for τ = 2, 3, 5. Software: Python 3.12.3, NumPy 2.5.3, SciPy 1.18.1, Matplotlib 3.11.1, pinned in `requirements.lock.txt`.

**The hypothesis and the rule.** The study was pre-specified in a git-tracked record (`manuscript/analysis_record.md`) as a directional test of whether whole-brain synergy (the ΦID atom sts under the Gaussian MMI estimator at lag one TR) is up-regulated under N,N-dimethyltryptamine, motivated by the synergistic-workspace account, in which loss of consciousness goes with reduced integrated information (ΦR) within the workspace (Luppi et al., 2024); the predicted direction was an increase, and the hypothesis extrapolates from that account rather than being its prediction. The hypothesis is the record's opening section, committed with the first scripts (44cec4f, 12 September 2026). A directional-failure rule — a significant decrease is reported as a refutation and not reframed — was written after a 115-region global fit had shown a decrease and entered the repository in the same commit as that result (33f0b33, 13 September 2026); the windowed estimator, the window sets, the nulls and the motion rule were fixed before the windowed result existed (e16ebba to e46df8a). The git record establishes ordering, not blindness; the full history is S5 Text.

**The windowed test.** Primary B: whole-brain mean MMI-sts in 14 non-overlapping 60-TR windows, model refit per window, the pre/post difference-in-differences (DiD) of windows 6–14 against 1–4, DMT minus placebo, per subject; exact sign-flip permutation over the 14 subjects, a subject-bootstrap 95 % CI (the pre-specified interval; the intervals quoted here invert the sign-flip test instead), a phase-randomised temporal null, framewise-displacement (FD) reporting and an FD-residualised recomputation. Result (`results/primary_b_ts_gsr_win60.csv`): DiD −0.0809 nats [−0.1317, −0.0310], sign-flip p = 0.0038, phase-randomised p = 0.0020, negative in 13 of 14 subjects, −7.0 % of the pre-injection DMT mean; FD-residualised −0.0649 [−0.1010, −0.0260], p = 0.0048; without global signal regression −0.1031 [−0.1629, −0.0435], p = 0.0026. Under the directional-failure rule this refuted the up-regulation hypothesis as operationalised, an increase of whole-brain MMI-sts (main text, Results 2). Total time-delayed mutual information fell with sts (TDMI DiD −0.1037 on ts_gsr).

**The intensity-tracking criterion and its controls.** The record ordered the possible claims in three tiers, fixed before any real windowed run (record, "Pre-registered analysis choices"): tier 1, whole-brain synergy follows the intensity time course step by step; tier 2, it correlates with intensity across the decay; tier 3, the pre/post step contrast plus the methods contribution. A tier-2 criterion (the group-mean sts series against the intensity ratings over the decay windows, |ρ| ≥ 0.80, with a time-in-scanner control on the placebo run and an FD-residualised recomputation that had to pass that control in full) was pre-specified with two void rules. The tracking was significant and negative, and it was void under its controls (S2 Table); the claimed tier is 3 (step contrast plus methods contribution). The ratings were collected in a later session of the same day than the analysed runs (Timmermann et al., 2023; Singleton et al., 2025), which is why they enter no result of the main text.

**The redundancy prediction.** Recorded before rtr was inspected: under a redundancy-dominance reading of an sts decrease, rtr should rise under DMT with a time course mirroring the sts decrease. It did not — on ts_gsr rtr fell with sts (DiD −0.0078 at W = 60, positive in 4 of 14 subjects; −0.0091 at the global fit, bins 9–14 against 1–8, as the record evaluated it), and the reading is unsupported on either variant (record, 84ea657).

**Exploratory regional analysis.** The regional DiD map (the mean over a region's pairs of the global-fit local sts) was negative in 114 of 115 regions on both variants; seven regions passed FDR on ts_gsr and 19 on ts_demean, all negative and all cortical, four common to both (S3 Table). The workspace comparison found no concentration of the decrease in the network proxy of the synergistic workspace: workspace minus non-workspace mean regional DiD −0.0095 [−0.0230, +0.0050] (percentile), p = 0.216, on ts_gsr and −0.0050 [−0.0225, +0.0120] (percentile), p = 0.590, on ts_demean, with the same picture under the named-subregion, subcortex-inclusive, gateway and broadcaster variants (S4 Table). The receptor-map correlations (five serotonin maps, spin test over 10,000 parcel rotations) passed no correction on either variant (S5 Table). All exploratory.

**EEG Lempel–Ziv check.** The prediction recorded before computation, that TDMI anti-correlates with EEG Lempel–Ziv complexity within the DMT run, held on both variants: per-subject Spearman ρ across 28 bins −0.2416 [−0.4011, −0.0765] (percentile), one-sided p = 0.0020 against the phase-randomised LZ null on ts_gsr, nothing on the placebo run (S6 Table; global functional connectivity per bin set in S7 Table). Both series are drug-locked, so the check establishes coherence across modalities, not tracking beyond the drug.

**Robustness across estimators.** The W = 30 positive control gave the same sign at 0.85 of the W = 60 magnitude (−0.0686 [−0.1142, −0.0242], p = 0.0042), and the pre-specified inconsistency criterion (opposite sign or larger magnitude) was not met. The global fit (Robustness A) gave −0.0801 [−0.1362, −0.0251], p = 0.0071, on ts_gsr and −0.1035 [−0.1784, −0.0267], p = 0.0132, on ts_demean. A placebo-fitted model applied out of sample to the DMT run (Robustness C) gave DMT − PCB −0.1110 [−0.1816, −0.0518], p = 0.0005, over bins 15–28, and a within-DMT step of −0.0508 [−0.1127, +0.0178], p = 0.1338. The sensitivity window set (5–14) is S1 Table; the post hoc proportionality check is S8 Table.

**What happened next.** An adversarial review on 14 September 2026, with its own computations, established that the decrease was carried by the self-prediction atoms and reproduced by the lag-1 autocorrelation contrast; the study was reframed as an account of the estimator (main text). Nothing above was relabelled by that reframing.
