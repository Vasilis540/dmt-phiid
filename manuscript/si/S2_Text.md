# S2 Text. The ΦR exploration and HRF deconvolution, and why it was closed

Supporting information for "The Gaussian-MMI synergy atom of integrated information decomposition is mostly self-prediction". Every result in this text is post hoc. Sources: `manuscript/analysis_record.md` (the closure entry of 14 September 2026, 12:48 UTC, commit b4f98a8); `notes/review_computations_2026-09-14.md`, section 3; `notes/regional_phir_deconv_2026-09-14.md`; `notes/review_results/inference_rows_deconv.csv`; `notes/review_results/logs/phir_baseline_and_slope.log`; `notes/review_results/logs/rev_extra.log`. Intervals of means over subjects are the inverted sign-flip intervals of S17 Table except where marked.

**ΦR on the raw series.** ΦR = TDMI − I(X;X′) − I(Y;Y′) + rtr, the quantity of the workspace account, with the full inference of the main text (Methods): on the primary variant and estimator it did not change under DMT (DiD +0.0007 [−0.0093, +0.0112], sign-flip p = 0.8971, phase-randomised p = 0.8372, every window set and trend correction null). In no variant × estimator combination was the primary-set sign-flip p below 0.05, and the three combinations with a phase-randomised p below 0.05 disagree in sign across variants (S15 Table).

**HRF deconvolution.** The regional series were deconvolved with rsHRF 1.7.0 (Wu et al., 2013, 2021) in a sandbox described in `notes/rev_deconv.py` (the deconvolved `.mat` merged under `notes/review_results/deconv/`, `scripts/01` rerun on it there; the resulting files carry `git=nogit`), and the pipeline was rerun unchanged. The deconvolution used rsHRF 1.7.0 with the toolbox defaults (`notes/rev_deconv.py`): the canonical HRF with temporal and dispersion derivatives (canon2dd), point-process events at local maxima above 1 SD (localK = 1 for TR ≤ 2 s), an onset search of 4–8 s, an HRF length of 24 s, microtime T = 3, AR(1) noise and a 0.01–0.08 Hz passband for the HRF estimate; one HRF per region per run, estimated from the whole run; classic non-iterative Wiener deconvolution; each series z-scored by the toolbox, so the deconvolved series are in those units. The estimated HRFs did not differ between runs at the group level (time to peak 6.08 s on DMT against 6.07 s on placebo, ts_gsr; `notes/review_computations_2026-09-14.md`, section 3). The deconvolved series and the sandbox are not in the repository; the four atom files computed from them (`notes/review_results/deconv/atoms_*.csv`) carry `git=nogit`, the deconvolved regional files under `notes/review_results/regional/` and `inference_rows_deconv.csv` carry no commit identifier, and `run_all.sh` regenerates them only when the sandbox is present. The numbers quoted from them can be checked against those files, and regenerated only by re-running `rev_deconv.py` with the parameters above on the released data. On ts_gsr at W = 60 (`inference_rows_deconv.csv`) deconvolution lowered whole-brain r₁ from 0.848 to 0.783 (DMT pre-injection) and the sts level from 1.155 to 0.832, and left the DMT contrast in place: sts DiD −0.0782 [−0.1221, −0.0362], sign-flip p = 0.0013, negative in 13 of 14, against −0.0809 on the raw series; the autocorrelation DiD after deconvolution is −0.0220 [−0.0342, −0.0101], p = 0.0021. The share of power inside 0.01–0.08 Hz and the run-level r₁ of the deconvolved series are in `rev_extra.log`. Deconvolution at TR 2 s therefore changes the operating point and not the mechanism (main text, Results 7); the evidence does not transfer to TR 0.72 s (S3 Text §10).

**The deconvolved ΦR increase and the regional test.** After deconvolution whole-brain ΦR rose under DMT (DiD +0.0178 [+0.0051, +0.0307], p = 0.0099, at W = 60 on ts_gsr), and a regional test with a prediction recorded before it was run was carried out (`notes/regional_phir_deconv_2026-09-14.md`). The increase supports no claim, for the reasons the closure entry records: a pre-injection baseline gap in the direction that creates it (deconvolved −0.0124, p = 0.014; raw −0.0042, p = 0.28); a placebo-run decline across the 14 windows (deconvolved slope −0.00115 per window, p = 0.025; raw −0.00036, p = 0.43); estimator disagreement; and a 56–64 % placebo share of the DiD. The within-pre-injection placebo decline (window 4 − window 1) is present on the raw series (−0.0084 [−0.0174, −0.0003], p = 0.039) as well as after deconvolution (−0.0120, p = 0.067) (`phir_baseline_and_slope.log`).

**The closure.** The exploration was closed by a dated entry (record, "Closure entry", 14 September 2026, 12:48 UTC), which records that no confirmatory claim attaches to a specification chosen after seeing results and that no further specification would be run in search of a positive DMT result. Nothing in it was relabelled afterwards; the Part B analysis plans of the estimator account (477cccc, 12:50 UTC) were written after the post hoc computations of that day existed (S5 Text).
