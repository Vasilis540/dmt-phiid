# S2 Text. The ΦR exploration and HRF deconvolution, and why it was closed

Supporting information for "The Gaussian-MMI synergy atom of integrated information decomposition is mostly self-prediction". Every result in this text is post hoc. Sources: `manuscript/analysis_record.md` (the closure entry of 14 September 2026, 12:48 UTC, commit b4f98a8); `notes/review_computations_2026-09-14.md`, section 3; `notes/regional_phir_deconv_2026-09-14.md`; `notes/review_results/inference_rows_deconv.csv`; `notes/review_results/logs/phir_baseline_and_slope.log`; `notes/review_results/logs/rev_extra.log`.

**ΦR on the raw series.** ΦR = TDMI − I(X;X′) − I(Y;Y′) + rtr, the quantity of the workspace account, with the full inference of the main text (Methods): on the primary variant and estimator it did not change under DMT (DiD +0.0007 [−0.0078, +0.0100], sign-flip p = 0.8971, phase-randomised p = 0.8372, every window set and trend correction null). In no variant × estimator combination was the primary-set sign-flip p below 0.05, and the three combinations with a phase-randomised p below 0.05 disagree in sign across variants (main text, Table 3).

**HRF deconvolution.** The regional series were deconvolved with rsHRF 1.7.0 (Wu et al., 2013, 2021) in a sandbox described in `notes/rev_deconv.py` (the deconvolved `.mat` merged under `notes/review_results/deconv/`, `scripts/01` rerun on it there; the resulting files carry `git=nogit`), and the pipeline was rerun unchanged. On ts_gsr at W = 60 (`inference_rows_deconv.csv`) deconvolution lowered the window-level r₁ from 0.848 to 0.783 (DMT pre-injection) and the sts level from 1.155 to 0.832, and left the DMT contrast in place: sts DiD −0.0782 [−0.1182, −0.0424], sign-flip p = 0.0013, negative in 13 of 14, against −0.0809 on the raw series; the autocorrelation DiD after deconvolution is −0.0220 [−0.0327, −0.0116], p = 0.0021. The share of power inside 0.01–0.08 Hz and the run-level r₁ of the deconvolved series are in `rev_extra.log`. Deconvolution at TR 2 s therefore changes the operating point and not the mechanism (main text, Results 7); the evidence does not transfer to TR 0.72 s (Discussion).

**The deconvolved ΦR increase and the regional test.** After deconvolution whole-brain ΦR rose under DMT (DiD +0.0178 [+0.0069, +0.0293], p = 0.0099, at W = 60 on ts_gsr), and a regional test with a prediction recorded before it was run was carried out (`notes/regional_phir_deconv_2026-09-14.md`). The increase supports no claim, for the reasons the closure entry records: a pre-injection baseline gap in the direction that creates it (deconvolved −0.0124, p = 0.014; raw −0.0042, p = 0.28); a placebo-run decline across the 14 windows (deconvolved slope −0.00115 per window, p = 0.025; raw −0.00036, p = 0.43); estimator disagreement; and a 56–64 % placebo share of the DiD. The within-pre-injection placebo decline (window 4 − window 1) is present on the raw series (−0.0084 [−0.0168, −0.0015], p = 0.039) as well as after deconvolution (−0.0120, p = 0.067) (`phir_baseline_and_slope.log`).

**The closure.** The exploration was closed by a dated entry (record, "Closure entry", 14 September 2026, 12:48 UTC), which records that no confirmatory claim attaches to a specification chosen after seeing results and that no further specification would be run in search of a positive DMT result. Nothing in it was relabelled afterwards; the Part B analysis plans of the estimator account (477cccc, 12:50 UTC) were written after the post hoc computations of that day existed (S5 Text).
