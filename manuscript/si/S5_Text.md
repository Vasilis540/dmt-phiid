## 1. History of the study

The analysis was pre-specified in a git-tracked record (`manuscript/analysis_record.md`; `manuscript/prespecification_summary.md` lists the decisions of the original analysis by commit, and covers that analysis only). The initial commit (44cec4f, 12 September 2026, 10:29 EEST (07:29 UTC)) carries the up-regulation hypothesis, the statement, heading its list of analysis choices, that they were 'Recorded here before any results were inspected', and a 20-region real-data global fit, whose file's modification time on V.S.'s disk was 10:12 EEST (07:12 UTC) that day, seventeen minutes before the commit (a time the review of 14 September 2026 read from the disk, `notes/adversarial_review_2026-09-14.md`, §3.1; git carries no timestamp for it), whose DiD is negative in 12 of 14 subjects (−0.0994 by the later pre/post definition; the file's header reads `git=nogit`, and `manuscript/prespecification_summary.md` does not mention it; review of 22 September 2026, Finding 6). The hypothesis predates that fit: it is the record's opening section, which precedes the data and method checks dated 11 September 2026 and so was written before any script existed (the scripts were first committed on 12 September). That ordering rests on document order within the record, not on a commit: the hypothesis and the fit entered git together, in the initial commit. The 20-region fit was run as a pipeline check under the script's "first" setting, which its docstring marks as a shape and sanity check of one contiguous chunk of cortex, never to be reported from. It was inspected and showed a decrease, and the record later calls it "the earlier 20-region observation". The initial commit carried both the hypothesis and that file, and the hypothesis was kept as the workspace account's prediction rather than revised to match a pilot the record had marked as unreadable (`manuscript/prespecification_summary.md`: "the direction was never revised"). The 115-region global fit's file carries a modification time of 10:15 EEST (07:15 UTC) on 13 September (the same source). The directional-failure rule, under which a significant decrease would be reported as a refutation, was written after it and entered the repository in the same commit as that result (33f0b33, 10:40 EEST (07:40 UTC)), which records "The direction is a DECREASE in synergy under DMT, opposite to the stated hypothesis" immediately above the rule. The sign-blind intensity-tracking criterion (84ea657, 11:07 EEST (08:07 UTC)) was adopted, in the record's words, because the 115-region fit showed a decrease and a sign-blind criterion could otherwise be gamed either way; the primary post set was moved from windows 5–14 to 6–14 (febf599, 11:32 EEST (08:32 UTC)) on real-data evidence from both arms of the global fit. The windowed test's estimator, window sets, nulls and motion rule were fixed before the windowed result existed (e16ebba to e46df8a), but the direction and approximate size of the effect were known from the global fit, whose per-subject DiDs correlate at 0.95 with the windowed ones; the git record establishes ordering, not blindness. The step contrast that became the primary contrast had no test on record until b7e4595 (13 September, 11:36 EEST (08:36 UTC)), after the 115-region fit: its statistic, like the directional-failure rule, was written after a decrease had been seen, which is why the paper calls it the primary contrast (review of 22 September 2026, Finding 6). The decrease was recorded (cb1b2cf, 13 September); the intensity-tracking claim was void under its controls. On 14 September an adversarial review with its own computations (the lag-1 autocorrelation contrast and its collinearity with sts, ΦR, the trend corrections, the sts-matched null, and a CCS decomposition with phyid's mask on 400 random pairs showing an sts increase in 14 of 14 subjects) established that the decrease was carried by the self-prediction atoms and reproduced by the autocorrelation contrast. The study was reframed. An exploration of ΦR, including HRF deconvolution and a regional test with a prediction recorded in advance, was closed by a dated entry (record, "Closure entry", 14 September 2026; commit b4f98a8, 12:48 UTC). That entry records why its positive whole-brain result supports no claim, that of the specifications chosen after seeing results "No confirmatory claim attaches to any of them", and that no further specification would be run in search of a positive DMT result. The Part B analysis plans (477cccc, 12:50 UTC) — in the plans' own names, scope map, CCS, lag dependence, diagnostic and literature table — were written after those review computations, so that the sign of every Part B outcome except the diagnostic's residual was known in outline before the plan was recorded. ## 2. The multiplicity count and the withdrawn weighting rule

The paragraph below is the Methods paragraph "Multiplicity" of the drafts of 15–20 September 2026, reproduced as it stood; the counts are those of the review and Part B result files at that time and do not include the computations of 20–21 September (B14–B20, B16b, B17b), whose inference rows are in `notes/review_results/inference_rows_prewhiten.csv` and the calibration tables.

**Multiplicity.** No correction is applied. The review and Part B result files (`notes/review_results/inference_rows_*.csv`) hold 474 inference rows: 79 quantity × variant × estimator combinations, each in six window sets (primary, sensitivity, early, late, two trend corrections); 186 rows are from the 14 September review's files (raw, deconvolved, W = 30) and 288 from Part B. Every row is a whole-brain DiD with its subject-bootstrap CI and sign-flip p; the phase-randomised null is carried by the 316 rows of the primary, sensitivity, early and late sets and not by the 158 trend-correction rows. The paper quotes the rows named in the text. Inferential weight is placed on the pre-specified primary contrast (MMI-sts, `ts_gsr`, W = 60) and on results whose sign-flip and phase-randomised p are both ≤ 0.005 across variants; a p between 0.01 and 0.06 (the residual DiD, 0.042; CCS-sts at W = 60 on `ts_gsr`, 0.056) is reported as such and is not treated as an established effect. This weighting rule is not a pre-specified rule. It first entered the manuscript at commit 9318997 (15 September 2026, 08:47 UTC), in the revision that followed the second adversarial review, after every result it governs existed (the Part B outcomes of 14 September and the published-definition CCS values recorded at 07:55 UTC on 15 September). It has no record entry of its own; it is a reporting convention adopted at write-up, stated here with its date.

**Withdrawal (decision D1 of 20 September 2026; record, "Decisions of 20 Sep 2026").** The rule was withdrawn in the restructuring of 21 September 2026. The paper now has one primary contrast, the pre-specified MMI-sts DiD on ts_gsr at W = 60; everything else is exploratory, reported with its effect size and interval and without threshold language. The rule's text and its history stay here. Under it, the two global-fit CCS-sts cells (sign-flip and phase-randomised p both ≤ 0.005 on both variants) would have been the only exploratory result treated as established; they were not read as a DMT finding under the rule either, for the reasons S3 Text §5 gives (main text, Results 6).
