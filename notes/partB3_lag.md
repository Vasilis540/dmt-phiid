## What was computed

At each τ the lag-τ 4-vector (x_t, y_t, x_{t+τ}, y_{t+τ}) replaces the lag-1 one in the closed-form Gaussian-MMI decomposition (`rev_phiid_fast.PairPhiID(X, tau=τ)`), per window (W = 60) and per run with 30-TR bins (global fit), whole-brain mean over the 6,555 pairs. The full engine (exact sign-flip, bootstrap CI, phase-randomised null on the TR-local series, FD residualisation, early/late, trend corrections) is run on the sts contrast at each τ and on the lag-τ autocorrelation contrast (`rev_series.autocorr_series(..., lag=τ)`: window-standardised mean regional lag-τ autocorrelation, the same construction as the review's lag-1 contrast). The scope map of B1 applies with r₁ → r_τ (the AR(1) 4×4 matrix has the same form with a replaced by the lag-τ autocorrelation), re-evaluated on r ∈ [−0.95, 0.95] because r_τ is negative at τ = 5.

## What it shows against the recorded prediction

1. r_τ falls with τ and the artefact weakens — as predicted. Mean lag-τ autocorrelation (DMT pre, window-standardised) 0.848, 0.513, 0.139, −0.202 for τ = 1, 2, 3, 5. sts level (W60, DMT pre) 1.155, 0.180, 0.026, 0.042 nats; |sts DiD| 0.081, 0.043, 0.0043, 0.0024. The τ = 5 level is above the τ = 3 level although |DiD| is smaller, because r₅ is negative and the model sts is an even function of the autocorrelation: for the AR(1) 4×4 matrix all 16 closed-form atoms are unchanged under a → −a (checked exactly, max difference 0.0, equal and unequal a_x, a_y), and at small |r| sts ≈ r² (0.0024, 0.0097, 0.0396 at r = 0.05, 0.1, 0.2 with |q| = 0.25). This is why the scope-map row for negative r in `lag_tables.md` mirrors the positive one.

2. The sts contrast persists while r_τ is itself changed by DMT and disappears when it is not. The lag-τ autocorrelation contrast is negative and significant at τ = 1, 2, 3 (−0.0146, −0.0468, −0.0681; p = 0.011, 0.0090, 0.013; larger in magnitude at the longer lags) and null at τ = 5 (−0.0108, p = 0.57). The sts DiD is negative and significant on both nulls, at both estimators, at τ = 1, 2, 3 (τ = 3, W60: −0.0043 [−0.0063, −0.0022], p = 0.0018, phase p = 0.0010, survives FD residualisation, 13–14/14 negative under the trend corrections; global −0.0116, p = 0.0012) and null at τ = 5 at both estimators (W60 −0.0024, p = 0.37; global +0.0015, p = 0.80). At τ = 5 the only cell with p < 0.05 is the early set at the global fit (+0.0102, p = 0.021, phase p = 0.090), positive, not present at W60 (early +0.0004, p = 0.88).

3. Per-subject tracking follows the sign of r_τ. The sts DiD correlates with the lag-τ autocorrelation DiD at +0.953 / +0.967 (τ = 1, W60 / global), +0.970 / +0.963 (τ = 2), +0.626 / +0.885 (τ = 3) and −0.832 / −0.912 (τ = 5). The sign reversal at τ = 5 is what the even dependence in point 1 gives: with r₅ negative at the pairs (median −0.275), a fall in r₅ moves the pairs away from zero and raises sts. The weaker correlation at τ = 3 goes with the pairs straddling zero at that lag (5–95 % range of pair r₃ −0.18 to +0.31): the whole-brain mean of an even function of r₃ does not follow the whole-brain mean of r₃. Correlations with the lag-1 autocorrelation DiD: +0.953 / +0.967, +0.952 / +0.962, +0.675 / +0.911, −0.371 / −0.671. The τ = 2 sts DiD correlates with the τ = 1 sts DiD at +0.991 (W60) / +0.998 (global); τ = 3 at +0.681 / +0.926; τ = 5 at −0.485 / −0.695.

4. Atom structure by lag. At τ = 2 the τ = 1 pattern is present at reduced scale (W60, DMT pre: rts = str = +0.089, mirror atoms xts, yts, stx, sty −0.058 to −0.059, sts +0.180 against xtx + yty +0.232). At τ = 3 and τ = 5 every atom other than xtx, yty and sts is within ±0.025 of zero at both estimators. At W60 the mirror atoms are ≈ 0 (|value| ≤ 0.0013), rts and str are +0.017 (τ = 3) and +0.023 (τ = 5), xty and ytx turn positive (+0.0125 at τ = 3, +0.0108 at τ = 5), and sts stays within 0.005 of xtx + yty (0.026 vs 0.025; 0.042 vs 0.045). At the global fit the mirror atoms are −0.004 (τ = 3) and −0.007 (τ = 5), xty and ytx stay negative (−0.0015, −0.0014), and sts is below xtx + yty by 0.014 (0.0156 vs 0.0292) and 0.010 (0.0230 vs 0.0328). The 16-atom DiD tables at τ = 3 and 5 are all within ±0.013 of zero at both estimators.

5. Whether any τ reaches the region where q contributes comparably: no. On the AR(1) family that region does not exist (B1: no q-dominated cell on r₁ ∈ [0, 0.95] × q ∈ [−0.6, 0.6] or |q| ≤ 0.95; re-evaluated here on r ∈ [−0.95, 0.95] × q ∈ [−0.95, 0.95], 191 × 191 cells: 0.0 % q-dominated, 0.5 % flat, the flat cells being the r = 0 row). The ratio |∂sts/∂r| / |∂sts/∂q| at |q| = 0.25 has its minimum 19.9 at r = 0.58 (6.6 at r = 0.61 for |q| = 0.6) and grows as r → 0 (28 at ±0.30, 40 at ±0.20, 78 at ±0.10), because ∂sts/∂r ≈ 2r while ∂sts/∂q vanishes faster; so lowering r_τ never makes q the dominant input. The real pairs are r-dominated at every lag (100, 100, 97.7, 99.5 %, the remainder flat), and the ratio at the pair medians is 32, 21, 97, 31. Per the pre-specification this is a limitation to state: the lag analysis cannot, at any τ, put the pairs where a q-driven synergy change would be distinguishable from an autocorrelation-driven one; what changing τ does is scale the artefact with r_τ (points 1–3) and remove it when DMT no longer changes r_τ (τ = 5).
