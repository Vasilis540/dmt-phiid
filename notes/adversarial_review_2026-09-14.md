# Adversarial review of `manuscript/draft.md` (state of 14 Sep 2026, commit a226593)

Scope: `draft.md`, `supplementary.md`, `prespecification_summary.md`, `figures/` read as one document; every claim checked against `results/*.csv`, the saved `.npy` arrays, `analysis_record.md`, the git history (all 42 commits), the scripts, and the two source papers. Where a number is not in any results file I computed it from the saved arrays; every such computation is in `notes/review_checks.py` and re-runs in about three minutes. The pipeline was also rebuilt from scratch in a fresh environment and re-executed as far as time allowed (details in section 0.2).

Verdict first. The empirical observation is real and reproducible: under Gaussian-MMI ΦID the whole-brain mean `sts` atom falls after DMT relative to placebo, in 13 of 14 subjects, and every number in the draft that is quoted from a results file is quoted correctly except the ones listed in section 2. The problems are in what the number means and in how the study is framed. The central claim is much weaker than the paper presents it, for three reasons that a reviewer from the group that invented the method will see at once: (i) `sts` on these data is a lattice artefact of the MMI redundancy function and is numerically a copy of within-region self-prediction, so "whole-brain synergy fell" is a restatement of "regional lag-1 autocorrelation fell" (r = 0.97 across subjects); the sign of the `sts` contrast flips to positive in 13–14 of 14 subjects under the other redundancy function phyid ships; and the quantity Luppi et al. actually reported, ΦR, does not change under DMT when computed from the paper's own atom tables; (ii) the "pre-specified test" was specified after the result was known from the global fit (per-subject r = 0.95 with the primary result), and the hypothesis itself was committed together with a real-data sanity run that already showed the decrease; (iii) the dataset is not what the paper says it is: the analysed run is the resting-state scan, and the intensity ratings come from a different scan of the same day.

## 0. Your two questions, and the rest of that category

### 0.1 The placebo rise

Yes, a reviewer catches it in Figure 1 before reading a word: the placebo trace climbs from 1.088 at window 5 to 1.193 at window 13 with two small reversals on the way, and the DMT trace climbs after window 6 and ends above its own baseline (window 14: DMT 1.1794 against a pre-injection mean of 1.1554; placebo 1.1782). Table 1 then states it (PCB post − pre +0.0324, p = 0.0425) and the text concedes "roughly 40 % of the DiD is the placebo rise". What the paper does not do is say what the rise is or show that the result does not depend on it. Three things the reviewer will do with it:

- Split the post set. On the windows in which the drug is present (6–9, bins 11–18, group-mean intensity 8.3 falling to 3.5) the DiD is −0.1029, p = 0.0009, 13/14 negative, and the placebo contributes 18 % of it (DMT −0.0842, PCB +0.0187). On the tail (windows 10–14, bins 19–28, intensity 2.2 falling to 0.6) the DiD is −0.0633, p = 0.034, 10/14, and the placebo rise is 69 % of it (DMT −0.0199, PCB +0.0434). Same on `ts_demean` (18 % vs 60 %). So the effect is carried by the drug-present windows and is robust there; the tail is mostly placebo drift. The paper should show this split; as written, the −0.0809 headline mixes a drug effect with a drift.
- Ask what the drift is. Mean regional lag-1 autocorrelation on the placebo run rises across the session (0.855 → 0.869, window 1 → 13, `review_checks.py` block 4), consistent with what drowsiness and settling do to band-passed BOLD, and exactly what raises `sts` under MMI (see 4.1). The DiD design assumes the placebo drift is additive and also present in the DMT run. If DMT suppresses the drift (keeps the subject aroused), the DiD attributes the missing drift to "synergy decrease". The paper's phase-randomised null does not address session-level drift at all; a per-subject linear time trend fitted to both runs, or the windows 6–9 restriction, would.
- Note the injection response in the placebo arm is as large as the DMT drop at window 5 (placebo 1.152 → 1.088, DMT 1.176 → 1.100) and that the placebo "rise" over windows 6–14 relative to windows 1–4 is partly a rebound from that dip. The paper excludes window 5 but keeps bin 8 in the baseline while stating in Methods that bins 8–10 are contaminated (5.2 below).

(2023): each testing day had two scanning sessions, the second repeating the procedure of the first including the injection; "This first session always consisted of continuous resting-state scans which lasted 28 min", and only in the second session were participants "(audio) cued to verbally rate the subjective intensity of drug effects every minute." ## 1. Claims in the draft not supported by the numbers in `results/`

Ordered by how much of the paper each one takes down.

**1.1 "Every entry is negative … no atom of consequence rose" (Results, Table 2 paragraph); "It was not a redistribution among atoms: total lag-one predictable information fell, and synergy carried the largest share" (Discussion); "synergy, being most of that information, falls with it" (Discussion).**
Table 2 shows six of sixteen atoms. The other ten are in `results/atoms_win60_115regions-all_ts_gsr_window.npy` (all 16 atoms, windows 6–14 vs 1–4, DiD): `xts` +0.0399, `yts` +0.0404, `stx` +0.0401, `sty` +0.0403. Each of these four sits at −0.535 nats at baseline (DMT pre-injection whole-brain means: `xts` −0.535, `yts` −0.536, `stx` −0.535, `sty` −0.536 against `sts` +1.155, `rts` +0.567, `str` +0.567). So the "synergy block" is `sts + rts + str = +2.29` offset by `−2.14` from four negative atoms, and under DMT the block's total change is −0.0017 of the −0.1037 TDMI change. The TDMI drop is `xtx` −0.0523 plus `yty` −0.0396 (self-transfer, i.e. within-region self-prediction) plus `rtr` −0.0078. The same holds in every cell: global `ts_gsr` block −0.012 of −0.122 (`xtx + yty` −0.098); global `ts_demean` block +0.018 of −0.132 (`xtx + yty` −0.157); windowed `ts_demean` block +0.020 of −0.119 (`xtx + yty` −0.143). On the no-GSR variant the synergy block rises while TDMI falls. Four atoms of consequence rose; synergy carried none of the total loss once its own mirrors are counted; the paper's atom-level story is the opposite of what the sixteen atoms say. Mechanism in 4.1.

**1.2 "The whole-brain mean of sts … falls when consciousness is lost under propofol and in disorders of consciousness, on the evidence of Luppi et al." (Discussion, Synergy and conscious level).**
Luppi et al. (2024) report edge-level reductions of ΦR concentrated in DMN gateway regions ("almost all regions showing consistent decreases in ΦR when consciousness was lost were members of the global synergistic workspace"); they report no whole-brain mean `sts`. The draft itself says two sentences earlier that "Luppi et al.'s collapsing quantity is integrated information (ΦR) within a workspace". ΦR from the paper's own atoms does not fall under DMT (0.3 above). The sentence that turns the result into an adjudication about conscious level is unsupported by the cited paper and contradicted by the paper's own data.

**1.3 "no concentration in a network proxy of the synergistic workspace" (Abstract); "the same picture under the named-subregion, subcortex-inclusive, gateway and broadcaster variants" (Results); "was not larger inside a network proxy of the workspace than outside it, on either variant, with either proxy" (Discussion).**
`results/regional_analysis_ts_gsr.csv`: `gateway_proxy_minus_nonworkspace_vs_noncortex_ws` −0.0189 [−0.0356, −0.0012], p = 0.0596, 10/14; with subcortex −0.0219 [−0.0399, −0.0028], p = 0.0466, 9/14; `gateway_proxy_minus_broadcaster_proxy` −0.0268 [−0.0514, −0.0007], p = 0.072. Both gateway CIs exclude zero and one sign-flip p is below 0.05; on `ts_demean` the same rows are null (−0.0094, p = 0.44; −0.0163, p = 0.21), and the named-subregion gateway proxy on `ts_gsr` is null (−0.0155, p = 0.20). So the evidence is a borderline, primary-variant-only signal — but it points toward a larger decrease inside the Default-network (gateway) proxy, and Luppi et al.'s propofol/DoC reduction is in DMN gateways specifically. That is not "the same picture" as the primary proxy's null; it is a hint of the pattern the paper says it has dissociated from, at the one proxy that matters for the comparison, and the draft's text hides it. The primary-proxy null (−0.0095, p = 0.216) arises because Default (larger decrease) and Control (smaller decrease, +0.0079 vs non-workspace) are pooled and cancel; Figure 3 shows the gradient. The honest sentence is "no significant concentration, with a non-significant trend toward the DMN gateway proxy on the primary variant".

**1.4 "the global fit did not show [the placebo rise] (global-fit placebo change over bins 9–14: −0.005)" (Results); "on real data it missed the placebo-run rise that the windowed estimator found" (Discussion, Estimator findings).**
`results/windowed_atoms_did_ts_gsr_win60.csv`, row `global / primary / sts`: `change_pcb` = +0.027961; `results/global_fc_did_ts_gsr.csv`: `PCB_post_minus_pre` sts +0.027961 [+0.0046, +0.0524], p = 0.0466. On the bins that match the primary contrast the global fit shows the placebo rise, significant. The −0.005 quoted is the peak-bin (9–14) change, a different bin set; the windowed figure it is set against (+0.0324) is on windows 6–14. The first of the three "estimator findings" claimed in the Discussion is false.

**1.5 "on … three estimators" (Discussion); "Robustness C confirms the sign under an independent model" (Results, Robustness).**
`results/robustness_c_ts_gsr.csv`: the within-DMT step, the only contrast under the placebo-fitted model that isolates the drug, is −0.0508 [−0.1047, +0.0137], p = 0.1338, 11/14. Contrast (ii) (−0.1110, p = 0.0005) compares DMT bins 15–28 (1.3213) with placebo bins 15–28 (1.4323) under the same model, but DMT bins 1–8 under that model, before any drug, are already 1.3549: a pre-drug offset of −0.077, which is 70 % of the −0.111. The draft reports the inflation asymmetry (+0.109 vs +0.052) but not that it puts the DMT baseline below the placebo held-out level. The record's own summary is "magnitude and within-DMT significance do not transfer"; the draft's is "confirms the sign". Two estimators, not three: the third's drug-isolating contrast is 11/14 and non-significant, and its 13/14 contrast carries a pre-drug offset of the same sign.

**1.6 "the group-mean sts series correlated with the intensity template at ρ_S = −0.9833 (p = 0.0020), passing the |ρ| ≥ 0.80 threshold" and "Control (a) cleared on the raw data" (Results, Intensity tracking).**
The thresholded statistic was never computed on the placebo run; control (a) is applied to the per-subject mean ρ instead. Computed on the placebo group-mean series with the same template (`review_checks.py` block 2): windows 6–14 ρ_S = −0.8333 (85 % of DMT's −0.9833); windows 5–14 −0.8788, larger in magnitude than DMT's −0.8667; `ts_demean` 5–14 −0.8303 against DMT's −0.6727. The placebo run passes the "threshold validated by simulation" too, and by the record's own void rule ("PCB … at least half its magnitude") the claim is void on the raw data at the level of the statistic the threshold applies to. The reason is trivial: over these windows the template is monotone in time, so Spearman against the template equals Spearman against the time index (identical values, block 2), and any monotone drift passes. The tier-2 threshold was moved from the per-subject statistic to the group-mean series at e46df8a; control (a) was not moved with it. The draft presents a pass that the placebo shares and a control cleared on a different statistic.

**1.7 "replicating the global-connectivity increase of Timmermann et al. (2023)" (Results, Mean pairwise correlation).**
`results/global_fc_did_ts_demean.csv`: DMT post − pre in mean r +0.0426 [+0.0006, +0.0872], p = 0.093; DiD +0.0526, p = 0.047, and only without GSR; with GSR the DiD is −0.0034, p = 0.0024. One p = 0.047 contrast, with the within-DMT change itself at p = 0.09 and the opposite sign on the primary variant, is not a replication.

**1.8 "with intensity rated every minute throughout" (Introduction); "with intensity ratings every 60 s" (Abstract); "rated … 28 times per run" (Methods).**
Contradicted by both source papers (0.3.1). Not a `results/` claim, but the claim the tracking design stands on.

**1.9 "subject 14, the only positive DiD on both variants" (Results, LOO).**
`results/loo_did_win60.csv`: `ts_demean` subject 2 `dropped_subject_did` = +0.003969. Two subjects are positive on `ts_demean` (12/14 negative, as the same paragraph says). Subject 14 is the only subject positive on both variants, which is presumably what was meant; as written the sentence reads as "the only positive DiD", which is false on `ts_demean`. Minor, but it is in the paragraph whose purpose is to show that no subject drives the result.

**1.10 "sts is the largest atom at the whole-brain mean in these data" (Introduction), used as a premise ("Less predictable dynamics mean less TDMI, and since sts is the largest atom … they mean less synergy").**
True as arithmetic and empty as an argument: `sts` is largest because MMI makes it a second copy of `xtx + yty` (4.1). Under CCS on the same pairs `sts` is −0.033 nats and TDMI is unchanged.

## 4. The three criticisms most likely from a hostile reviewer at a Q1 journal, and what would answer them

### 4.1 "Your synergy is an MMI artefact; it is regional autocorrelation with a different name, and Luppi's quantity does not move."

On these pairs (lag-1 autocorrelation ≈ 0.86, mean |r| between regions ≈ 0.20) the Gaussian MIs are, for a typical pair: I(X;X′) = 0.668, I(Y;Y′) = 0.666, cross terms ≈ 0.03, I(X;X′Y′) = 0.672, I(Y;X′Y′) = 0.670 (`calc_PhiID` intermediates, 300 random pairs, subject 1 DMT). MMI sets the redundancy of X and Y about the joint future to min(0.672, 0.670) ≈ 0.67 — it cannot tell "X predicts X′ and Y predicts Y′ by the same amount" from "X and Y carry the same information" — and the Möbius inversion pays for that 0.67 nats of phantom redundancy with `rts ≈ str ≈ +0.6`, four mixed atoms at ≈ −0.6 each, and `sts ≈ +1.24 ≈ xtx + yty`. The whole-brain `sts` of 1.2–1.3 nats is a shadow of self-prediction, and it moves with self-prediction:

- Mean regional lag-1 autocorrelation per 60-TR window (one line of numpy, no ΦID): DMT 0.863 → 0.853, placebo 0.860 → 0.866; DiD −0.0155, p = 0.006, 12/14. Per-subject correlation with the `sts` DiD r = 0.973; across the 28 condition × window group means r = 0.984.
- Under CCS redundancy on the same 400 random pairs, same subjects, same model: `sts` baseline −0.033 nats (i.e. zero), DiD +0.0212, positive in 14/14, p = 0.0001 (`ts_gsr`); +0.0330, 14/14 (`ts_demean`); an independent 400-pair draw gives +0.0216, 13/14, p = 0.0002 and +0.0324, 13/14. TDMI DiD identical to MMI (−0.1189), because TDMI does not depend on the redundancy function. The sign of the headline depends on a choice the paper does not mention. (CCS has its own known problems and phyid's CCS double-redundancy is marked "to be implemented" in the source; the point is not that CCS is right, it is that the sign is a property of the redundancy function.)
- ΦR, the quantity Luppi et al. report, computed from the saved atoms: DiD −0.006 (p = 0.095), +0.001 (p = 0.90), +0.018 (p = 0.12), +0.016 (p = 0.085) across the four cells (0.3.3).

Two further things the same reviewer will raise. Luppi et al. deconvolved the haemodynamic response from the regional BOLD before ΦID ("we used a dedicated toolbox to deconvolve the hemodynamic response function from our regional BOLD signal timeseries prior to analysis"); this paper does not. HRF smoothing inflates lag-1 autocorrelation, which is the quantity that drives `sts` under MMI, so the two studies' `sts` values are not on the same footing even before the state comparison. And Luppi et al. compute one Gaussian per pair over the whole scan on 454 parcels, whereas the paper's primary estimator refits on 59 samples; the paper's own simulations say the per-window bias depends on the covariance (record: "it is biased by a different amount under each covariance"), and its Discussion nonetheless calls the bias "common to both conditions" on the strength of the pre-injection gap alone.

What the paper would need: (i) report all sixteen atoms and say that four are negative and rise; (ii) compute ΦR and the synergy-minus-redundancy rank gradient of Luppi et al., since those are the objects the Introduction and Discussion talk about; (iii) repeat the primary contrast with at least one other redundancy function (CCS is already in phyid) and with a null model for atom values (Liardi et al. 2024 — cited in the draft — exists for exactly this purpose); (iv) run the sts-matched null the record itself listed as a precondition; (v) report the lag-1 autocorrelation contrast beside the `sts` contrast and say what `sts` adds to it. If nothing survives (ii)–(iv), the honest paper is "DMT reduces the lag-1 self-predictability of regional BOLD, consistent with the entropic-brain and Lempel-Ziv literature; Gaussian-MMI ΦID re-expresses this as a synergy decrease and should not be read as one", which is a smaller and publishable methods paper. The Cambridge group and the Imperial group both know the MMI negativity issue; presenting `sts` as "most of the information" to them will not survive the first read.

### 4.2 "This is not a pre-specified test; the outcome was known before the rules were written."

Section 3.1. What would answer it: rewrite every pre-specification claim to state what was known when, in the paper, not only in the record; drop "pre-specified test" from the title; present the windowed analysis as a confirmatory re-estimate under fixed rules of an effect first seen in the global fit; and either remove the Robustness-A-derived window-5 exclusion from the "primary" or show the 5–14 result with equal prominence (it is in S1 and is fine). Disclose the AI co-authorship and the purpose statement in the record, or curate the public record. Also address the counter-reading that a hypothesis committed alongside an opposite-signed sanity run is a pre-registered refutation by construction; the only defence is that the run was not read before the commit, and nothing in the repository can establish that.

### 4.3 "The design cannot separate drug from session drift, motion and injection, and the ratings are from another scan."

The pieces: the placebo drift and its 40 % share (0.1); DMT recovering to the placebo level by window 14; FD 0.203/0.173/0.161 at windows 5–7 against a 0.117–0.132 baseline, with 5.2, 3.4 and 3.4 frames per window above the 0.4-mm scrubbing threshold in those windows on DMT (against 0.4–0.9 pre-injection and 0.4–2.7 later; Singleton et al. replaced such frames with the mean of their neighbours before release), which touches lag-1 structure directly and is not captured by a linear FD regression; an injection response at bins 8–10 in both arms, with bin 8 inside the baseline; a 14-window per-subject FD regression that the paper itself calls crude and that removes 20 % of the effect; ratings from a separate session (0.3.1); N = 14 with one subject at −0.28 (the paper's LOO handles this adequately). What would answer it: the windows 6–9 split; a per-subject time-trend model or a placebo-drift-corrected contrast; per-window scrubbed-frame counts as a covariate or a sensitivity excluding windows with > k interpolated frames; a baseline of windows 1–3; the correct description of the ratings; and a statement of the counterbalanced order.

## 7. Writing

**Claims stronger than the evidence.**
- Title: "a pre-specified test of two accounts of synergy and conscious level". Neither "pre-specified" (3.1) nor "conscious level" (1.2, 4.1) is earned.
- Abstract: "dissociates the DMT state from the workspace-concentrated collapse reported under anaesthesia" — from an exploratory analysis, on a network proxy with Jaccard 0.32 against the data's own rank rule, with the gateway proxy pointing the other way (1.3).
- Discussion: "The two accounts … made opposite predictions for the same measured quantity, and the data decided between them." The measured quantity is not the one either account is about (4.1).
- Conclusion: "Synergy at this scale does not index conscious level; it indexes predictability" — a definitional claim from N = 14, one drug, one redundancy function, one parcellation.
- Discussion: "in 12–13 of 14 subjects, on two nulls, two window sets, two preprocessing streams and three estimators" — not on the third estimator (1.5).
- "replicating" (1.7); "uniformly" (5.3); "no atom of consequence rose" (1.1).

**Hedging that reads as evasion.**
- "it stands as written" (Results, tracking) — defensive; delete.
- "The honest summary is …" (Discussion) — a paper does not certify its own honesty; state the summary.
- "is not a licensed sentence" (Discussion) — record jargon; say "we do not claim that …".
- "the record was written over two days, so its ordering, not its duration, is what the audit trail establishes" (Limitations) — the thing to say is that the outcome was known from the global fit before the windowed rules were written.
- "fixed in a git-tracked record before any windowed result existed" (Abstract) — the qualifier "windowed" is doing all the work and no reader will weigh it.
- "(exploratory)" appended to every clause of the regional Results — once per paragraph, or once in the heading, is enough; repetition reads as a legal disclaimer.

**Unclear passages.**
- "Bias characterisation and the choice of window length" and "Effective sample size" are written for someone who has read the record. "Tier 1/2/3", "M = 1,338", "S", "R", "control (a)/(b)", "Robustness A/B/C", "the deciding pooling" are undefined in the paper. Either define once or cut.
- Results, Table 2 paragraph: "sts carried the largest share, the within-region self-transfer atoms xtx and yty most of the rest" — a reader cannot know what a self-transfer atom is; and the sentence is wrong (1.1).
- Methods, Intensity tracking: "Tracking is assessed on |ρ_S|; the sign is reported separately against the pre-specified positive direction" — explain in one clause why a sign-blind criterion was adopted (the record does: the decrease was already visible).
- Methods, Robustness C: the reader is told both evaluations are out-of-sample but not that the fit half contains the injection.
- "The estimator is characterised … on simulated VAR(1) processes with analytic ground truth" followed by "no simulated family matches the real dependence structure" — the reader needs to be told in one sentence what the simulations are then for.
- Data section: the subject codes (the 14 codes, not reproduced here; removed for data governance on 15 September 2026) are participant identifiers from another study's supplementary object; whether they should be reproduced in a paper is a question for the data owners.

**Small things.** "[TK]" placeholders in the affiliation, the PET atlas source, the licence and the acknowledgements; "Independent researcher, Athens" as sole affiliation on a paper going to a collaborator who will presumably be an author; the reference list's own note that author lists and pages are unverified.
