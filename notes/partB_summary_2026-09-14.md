## What each item showed

B1 (scope map, no prediction). On the bivariate AR(1) family, |∂sts/∂r₁| > |∂sts/∂q| at every grid point except r₁ = 0, on r₁ ∈ [0, 0.95] × q ∈ [−0.6, 0.6] and on |q| ≤ 0.95: there is no q-dominated region. The ratio is 32 at the data's operating point (0.85, 0.25), with a grid minimum of 6.55 at (0.61, ±0.6) (corrected from the original note). sts = xtx + yty exactly on q = 0, within 1–2 % at |q| = 0.2. 100 % of subject 1's 6,555 pairs (both variants, both runs, windows 1–4 and 6) lie in the r₁-dominated region; the autocorrelation DiD projected through the map gives −0.087 nats against the observed −0.081.

B2 (MMI or Gaussian PID; prediction recorded). The MATLAB reference implements MMI only; phyid's CCS is Ince's single-target CCS plus a double co-information mask (D ≡ rtr − sts), verified against an independent implementation (2e-13) and a binary hand case (3e-15), with the published definition of the double-redundancy step not retrievable in this session (not checked). On the data the CCS sts is small and negative (−0.04 to −0.03 against +1.1 to +1.3 for MMI), does not resemble xtx + yty, and its DiD is uncorrelated with the autocorrelation contrast (r = −0.44 to −0.21, p ≥ 0.12); xtx and yty keep their MMI values and DiDs (r = 0.91–0.96 with r₁). By the rule fixed in advance: MMI-specific, claim narrow. The positive CCS sts DiD (+0.0036 to +0.0321) is, as pre-recorded, not evidence of anything.

B3 (lag; prediction recorded). r_τ falls with τ (0.848, 0.513, 0.139, −0.202 at τ = 1, 2, 3, 5), the sts level falls to τ = 3 (1.155, 0.180, 0.026) and is 0.042 at τ = 5 because r₅ is negative and the atoms are even in r_τ, and |DiD| falls throughout (0.081, 0.043, 0.0043, 0.0024): as predicted. The sts DiD is significant on both nulls wherever the lag-τ autocorrelation contrast is (τ = 1, 2, 3) and null where it is not (τ = 5). Per-subject r(sts DiD, r_τ DiD) = +0.95, +0.97, +0.63, −0.83 (W60; global +0.97, +0.96, +0.89, −0.91); the sign follows the sign of r_τ because every AR(1) atom is even in a (exact). No τ moves the pairs into a q-comparable region, because none exists on the family (0 of 191 × 191 cells on [−0.95, 0.95]²): stated as the limitation the pre-specification named.

B4 (diagnostic; prediction recorded). The diagonal-VAR(1) prediction from each pair's measured (a_x, a_y, q) reproduces the observed sts level to 4.3 % (1.1 % at the run level), the per-subject observed DiD at r = 0.99, and over-predicts the primary DiD by 14 % (−0.0924 vs −0.0809; 18–29 % on ts_demean and at W = 30). The residual DiD, +0.0115 [+0.0021, +0.0211], p = 0.042, phase p = 0.019, is neither "near zero" nor "uncorrelated with r₁" (r = −0.60 to −0.82): neither pre-specified outcome as worded; the signature of an autocorrelation-independent component is not met. A post-hoc supplement (ts_gsr W60 only, labelled as such) locates the whole residual in the cross-lag substitution corr(x_t, y_{t+1}) → a_y q (pair-level scatter SD 0.113 about a zero-mean deviation); the residual is more negative at higher a and |q|, and the positive residual DiD is that over-prediction shrinking as a and |q| fall under DMT. The τ = 1 atoms depend on lag-0 and lag-1 correlations only.

B5 (literature, no prediction). Eleven rows; two rows and one further source inaccessible, one Methods section truncated. Varley 2024 (arXiv) is prior art: the same phenomenon shown by disintegration at fixed autocorrelation on toy systems and one HCP subject, without the derivative map, the operating point, the lag dependence or the per-pair prediction. None of the other accessible fMRI ΦID studies addresses autocorrelation. Four are in scope as far as determined (Gatica 2024; Down 2026; Nago 2026; Dong 2025: Gaussian-MMI synergy on BOLD, TR 2–3 s, no deconvolution mentioned; implied r₁ 0.93, 0.85, 0.78 for the first three, band not stated for Dong). Luppi 2022 (TR 0.72 s, outside the pre-specified range) replicates without deconvolution; Luppi 2022/2023/2024 deconvolve in their primary analyses; Luppi 2024/2026 report ΦR. Family facts for the readings: rtr + sts = TDMI (a q change moves synergy and redundancy oppositely by equal amounts; an r₁ change moves sts and self-prediction together, rtr ≈ 1 % as much) and ΦR ≡ rtr = −½ ln(1 − r₁²q²) on the symmetric family.

## The five limitation statements

1. Windowed-estimator manufacture (review item 5: a covariance change at equal analytic sts manufactures −9 % to +16 % of the real DiD at W = 60, up to +33 % at W = 30). Partly answered. B4 shows the windowed contrast is what the closed form returns for the windows' own measured lag-1 structure, to within 14 % (over-prediction), per subject at 0.99, and B3 shows a contrast of the same sign and significance at the global fit at every τ ≤ 3. Still to concede: the finite-sample bias bound itself is unchanged (≤ 16 % at W = 60, either sign), and the over-prediction of the DiD is larger at W = 30 (27–29 %).

2. AR(1) mismatch (the real ACF decays faster than AR(1) beyond lag 1). Answered, with one concession. The observed sts and its contrast use the measured 4 × 4 matrix and no AR(1) assumption; the τ = 1 quantities depend only on lag-0 and lag-1 correlations, so the ACF beyond lag 1 does not enter them (B4, point 5); the map's reading holds at τ = 2 (r = +0.97) and, with the straddling-zero qualification, at τ = 3 (r = +0.63 W60, +0.89 global), where the ACF departs from AR(1) (B3). To concede: the map's q-axis and the B4 prediction assume the AR(1) cross-lag structure corr(x_t, y_{t+1}) = a_y q, and that substitution costs 3.5–9.7 % of the level and 14–29 % of the DiD across the four windowed cells (4 % and 14 % at ts_gsr W60).

3. MMI-specificity. Answered by B2 under the pre-recorded rule: the artefact is MMI-specific, the claim narrow. To concede: two redundancy functions examined (MMI; phyid's CCS, whose double-redundancy step has no published text checked here), Gaussian and continuous only; the discrete CCS on binarised data of Luppi 2023 was not examined.

4. Lag. Not answered; stated as the limitation the pre-specification named, now with numbers: no lag separates a q-driven synergy change from an autocorrelation-driven one, because the family has no q-dominated region and the pairs are r-dominated at every τ; what τ does is scale the artefact with r_τ and remove it when r_τ is not itself changed (τ = 5). To concede: τ > 1 is not a route to an autocorrelation-free sts, and at lags where r_τ straddles zero the reading is non-linear (sts even in r_τ).

5. Applicability. Partly answered by B5. The estimator and the absence of an autocorrelation control are documented for the four in-scope studies; the operating point is documented where the band and TR are stated (implied r₁ 0.78–0.93 for Gatica, Down, Nago; not for Dong) and assumed for the Luppi 2022 non-deconvolved replication (TR 0.72 s, band not stated, outside the pre-specified TR range); on this repository's data HRF deconvolution leaves r₁ at 0.79 and the structure intact, so the mechanism is not removed by deconvolution here. Still to concede: (i) whether any published effect is an autocorrelation change cannot be decided without those studies' r₁ (the map gives the sign only given the sign of the r₁ change, which none of them reports; for AD/SZ/ASD/ADHD/TUS the direction is not established here); (ii) the sts result does not extend to ΦR (ΦR ≡ rtr on the family, q-sensitive), so Luppi 2024/2026 are outside it; (iii) Tarchi 2026 and Mediano 2025 were inaccessible and the Luppi 2026 Methods were truncated.

Net: 3 answered under the pre-recorded rule (with its scope concession); 2 answered with a stated concession; 4 not answered — it is the stated limitation, now quantified; 1 and 5 partly answered, with the bias bound and the per-study verdicts remaining concessions.
